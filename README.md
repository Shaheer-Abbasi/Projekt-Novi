Projekt Novi is a high-Octane 2D rougelike with Gacha elements.

## Story

#Projekt Novi,” an unauthorized project by an unnamed Corporation in a futuristic dream city clouded with neon lights. 
Its purpose: enhancing unknowing novice Mercenaries to test out newly made cybernetic implants. 
Each Mercenary were tailored to their specific strengths: Hacking, Assassinating, and creating a being impervious to damage.
Not all who enrolled into the program survived, nor were the number accounted for, simply being listed as “whereabouts unknown.” 
Few who escaped now fight to survive the corrupted city, where the bright, neon lights serve more as a warning than a beacon of dreams.


## Game Engine

We will be using the [GoDot](https://godotengine.org/) engine due to its easy learning curve for beginners and being open source.

## Inputs

### Keyboard Controls

- W A S D - Generic keys for movement: forward, left, right, and backwards.
- Mouse - Aim in a direction to attack
- 1 - Ability 1
- 2 - Ability 2
- 3 - Ability 3
- 4 - Ultimate Ability
- E - Interact
- Q - N/A
- Shift - Dash/Dodge

### Controller Support

- Left Analog Stick - Move forward, left, right, and backwards
- Right Analog Stick - Aim in a direction
- L1 - Ability 1
- R1 - Ability 2
- R3 - Ability 3
- R3 + L3 - Ultimate Ability
- X - Interact
- O - N/A
- L3 - Dodge
